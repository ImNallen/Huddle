# Design System: Huddle

## Overview

**Creative North Star: "The Known Floor Plan"**

A Discord user should know where everything is at first sight. Huddle deliberately follows Discord's desktop shell, at the user's request, and plays it straight: a rail of rounded-square tiles, a channel sidebar, a flexible chat, a member list, a composer at the bottom, and an account panel floating across the rail and sidebar. Recognition is the design. Nothing here competes with that layout for attention, and the shell asks nothing of the people chatting in it.

Huddle keeps its own name and logo and uses no Discord logo, art, or font. The typeface is Figtree, bundled through `@fontsource-variable/figtree`. The result is a dense, flat, near-black room: a graphite shell around a slightly lifted chat ground, one indigo accent, and full-width messages with no bubbles. It replaces Huddle's earlier quiet SaaS arrangement of breadcrumbs, bubbles, and a centered message column.

The interface shows only what exists. Controls for unbuilt features (voice calls, search, emoji, attachments, presence) are deliberately absent, so a familiar layout never promises something the server cannot do. Voice channels are the one exception: they can be created and listed, and opening one says voice isn't available yet, with no call controls. The theme follows the system. Dark values were sampled from Mobbin captures of Discord; the light palette is derived, with no reference.

**Key Characteristics:**

- A fixed floor plan that a Discord user reads without learning it.
- Flat, full-width messages on a slightly lifted ground inside a darker shell.
- One indigo accent for action, focus, and the current place on the rail.
- Hairlines and tonal steps for structure; shadows only where something floats.
- Figtree throughout, with a tight type ramp and a 14px interface base.
- Only built features have controls.

## Colors

A near-black graphite neutral family carries one saturated indigo, with status colors reserved for state and eight bright mascot colors reserved for people. All values live in `src/chat.css`; the token suffix names the mode, and tokens without a suffix are shared by both.

### Primary

- **Huddle Indigo** (#5865f2): Primary buttons, the current or hovered rail tile, input focus borders, the composer send icon, the text caret, and selection tint. White text sits on it (#ffffff).
- **Pressed Indigo** (#4752c4): Primary button hover. It also serves as the light-mode link color.
- **Focus Ring** (dark #8a93f6, light #5865f2): The 2px keyboard focus outline on controls and fields. The composer's text area is the one exception; see Composer.
- **Link Periwinkle** (dark #afbeed, light #4752c4): Text links and quiet actions such as "Change".
- **Mention Tint** (text: dark #c9cdfb, light #3c45a5; fill: 30% indigo in dark, 15% indigo in light): @mentions in messages and the accent tag.

### Secondary

Status colors, not a second brand voice.

- **Connected Green** (dark #23a55a, light #1c8548): The account panel's connected dot, finished setup steps and the copied state.
- **Waiting Amber** (dark #f0b232, light #a96a00): The account panel's not-connected dot and warning notices.
- **Soft Danger** (dark #f87171, light #c02e33): Error text, failed messages, invalid input borders, and destructive actions.
- **Count Red** (#da373c): The unread count badge on the rail, with white numerals.

### Tertiary

- **Mascot Set** (indigo #818cf8, sky #38bdf8, teal #2dd4bf, lime #a3e635, amber #fbbf24, orange #fb923c, rose #fb7185, violet #c084fc): The eight colors a person can give their mascot avatar. They mark people and appear on no other surface. They are Huddle's own, carried in `src/Avatar.tsx`.

Server badges are tinted by hash: a hue from a set of eight, with lightness set per mode by the badge tokens (text at 72% lightness in dark and 34% in light; fill at 18% and 90%). Fallback initial avatars use the same lightness at a fixed hue of 250.

### Neutral

- **Graphite Shell** (`chrome`: dark #131215, light #e3e5e8): The ground behind the rail and title bar.
- **Channel Column** (`sidebar`: dark #131215, light #f2f3f5): The channel sidebar, member list, and settings side column. In dark it matches the shell and is separated by a hairline only.
- **Chat Ground** (`bg`: dark #1b1a1f, light #ffffff): The main column, one step above the shell.
- **Account Panel Ground** (`panel`: dark #212025, light #ebedef): The floating account panel and the Settings window ground. `rail-tile` matches it in dark (#212025) and is white in light (#ffffff).
- **Raised Surface** (`raised`: dark #242328, light #ffffff): Menus, tooltips, and dialogs.
- **Card Surface** (`card`: dark #1c1b20, light #ffffff): Cards, tables, and setup steps. In dark it is nearly the chat ground, so the hairline border does the work.
- **Composer Fill** (`input`: dark #212327, light #ebedef): The composer and the segmented control.
- **Secondary Fill** (`secondary`: dark #2a292e, light #e3e5e8; hover dark #38373d, light #d7d9dc): Secondary buttons, tags, and copy controls.
- **Icon Tile** (`tile`: dark #393a40, light #dfe1e5): Round channel marks, the access symbol, and server tiles on access screens.
- **Washes** (`hover`, `selected`, `message-hover`): Translucent white in dark and translucent near-black in light, at 5%, 11%, and 4% in dark and 5%, 9%, and 3% in light. They layer over whichever ground is below them.
- **Hairlines** (`line`, `line-strong`): 7% and 13% white in dark, 9% and 16% near-black in light. Panel edges, dividers, and field borders.
- **Text ramp** (`strong`: dark #ffffff, light #060607; `text`: dark #f2f2f3, light #1f2125; `muted`: dark #a09fa4, light #5c5e66; `faint`: dark #96959a, light #5c5e66; `placeholder`: dark #94969b, light #5c5e66): Names and headings, then message text, then secondary text, then timestamps. Light mode collapses the bottom three steps into one value to hold contrast.
- **Access Field** (`access-field-from`: dark #1a1650, light #262a8c; `access-field-to`: dark #2b52d2, light #2a54c8; at 160 degrees): The blue gradient behind setup, join, and the screens that explain a missing identity.
- **Access Card** (`access-card`: dark #323339, light #ffffff; `access-input`: dark #27292d, light #ebedef): The lifted card on the access field and its filled inputs.
- **Scrim** (70% black dark, 55% white light): The backdrop of every dialog, which also blurs the app behind it by 8px.

### Named Rules

**The Sampled Ground Rule.** Dark values were sampled from Mobbin captures of Discord. The light palette is derived, with no reference, so check a light value by contrast and by capture rather than by comparison to a source.

**The One Accent Rule.** Indigo marks action, focus, and the current place on the rail. It is never a panel ground, and no second accent exists.

## Typography

**Display Font:** Figtree Variable (with -apple-system, BlinkMacSystemFont, SF Pro Text, Inter, and system-ui as fallbacks)
**Body Font:** Figtree Variable
**Label/Mono Font:** ui-monospace (with SF Mono, SFMono-Regular, and Menlo as fallbacks), for hostnames, invite codes, and Tailscale logins

**Character:** One friendly geometric sans at every level, bundled through `@fontsource-variable/figtree`, so the familiar layout does not depend on Discord's proprietary face. Hierarchy comes from weight and color more than size.

### Hierarchy

- **Display** (700, 32px, 1.25): The channel welcome heading only, dropping to 24px at 760px wide.
- **Headline** (700, 24px, 1.25): Home greeting and the Members page.
- **Headline Access** (600, 24px, 1.25): The centered heading on the access card.
- **Title Dialog** (700, 20px): Centered dialog headings.
- **Title** (600, 16px): Channel name in the top bar, the current tab in the Settings window's top bar, server name in the sidebar, message authors, and card headings, including "Direct messages" on Home.
- **Body** (400, 16px, 1.375): Message text and the composer. Lines run the full width of the chat column.
- **Nav** (500, 15px): Channel rows, member rows, and settings navigation.
- **UI** (400, 14px, 1.375): The interface base, set on the root. Weight 500 (`ui-strong`) for buttons, menu items, tabs, and field labels.
- **Section Label** (500, 12px, mixed case): "Text Channels", member group headings, and the "Settings" label. Table headers use the same size at 600.
- **Meta** (400, 12px, tabular numerals): Timestamps and counts, in `faint` or `muted`. The hover-revealed continuation time uses 11px.
- **Access Label** (700, 12px, 0.02em, uppercase): Field labels on the access card and the saved servers list label.

### Named Rules

**The One Family Rule.** Figtree sets everything people read as language. Monospace appears only for machine values such as hostnames and invite codes.

**The Quiet Label Rule.** Shell labels stay 12px and mixed case. Uppercase belongs to field and list labels inside the access card, and nowhere in the shell.

## Layout

The shell is a fixed floor plan on a CSS grid filling the viewport below the window bar (minimum window height 480px): 72px rail, 248px channel sidebar, flexible chat, and 240px member list, all under a 32px title bar. In the browser it is a grid row showing the server. In the desktop app `WindowBar` replaces it with a fixed bar over every screen, and `:root[data-native]` collapses the row and sets `--window-bar-height`.

The sidebar header and top bar share a 48px height, so one horizontal line runs across the top of the shell. The account panel is absolutely placed 8px from the bottom left, spanning the rail and sidebar minus 16px; the rail stops 72px above the window bottom and the sidebar reserves 80px of bottom padding, so nothing scrolls under it.

The thread runs full width with no measure: messages are inset 72px on the left to clear the 40px avatar, and 48px on the right. Pages within the chat column are narrower by purpose: Home is a centered 680px column, its setup state 440px; Members is a left-aligned table up to 880px. The Settings window centers its content in a column of up to 680px. Dialogs are 460px and the access card 480px.

Spacing runs on 8px with 2, 4, 6, 12, and 20px steps for tight assemblies. Density is high: nav rows are 32px, and author groups are separated by 16px while continuation lines stack with none.

Responsive changes are progressive. At 1000px the member list and its toggle disappear. At 760px the rail narrows to 56px, the sidebar to 200px, rail tiles to 36px, and the Settings side column to 168px. At 560px the access header shrinks, and the Settings window fills the space below the window bar with an 8px margin and stacks under a horizontal nav. At 480px the rail is 48px, the sidebar 130px, and the rail tile 32px, and Home's conversation rows drop their avatars and unread tags.

## Elevation & Depth

A hybrid that is flat by default. The shell is built from tonal steps and hairlines; the chat ground sits one step above the graphite shell, and the top-left corner of the sidebar is rounded 8px so the content area reads as a sheet set into the chrome. Shadows appear only on things that float above the shell, and on the card lifted off the access field. The account panel floats by position, with a hairline border and no shadow.

### Shadow Vocabulary

- **Menu** (`box-shadow: 0 8px 24px rgb(0 0 0 / 0.4)` dark, `0 8px 24px rgb(6 6 7 / 0.16)` light): Server menu and rail tooltips.
- **Modal** (`box-shadow: 0 16px 48px rgb(0 0 0 / 0.5)` dark, `0 16px 48px rgb(6 6 7 / 0.24)` light): Dialogs, over the scrim.
- **Access Card** (`box-shadow: 0 4px 16px rgb(0 0 0 / 0.35)` dark, `0 4px 16px rgb(6 6 7 / 0.2)` light): The card on the access field.

### Named Rules

**The Flat Shell Rule.** Rail, sidebar, chat, member list, and account panel carry no shadow. A shadow means the element is temporarily above the shell or sits on the access field.

## Shapes

Gently rounded rectangles at a small set of radii: 4px for menu items, mentions, and tags; 6px for rows, tabs, and icon buttons; 8px for buttons, inputs, cards, the composer, and the account panel; 12px for dialogs. People and channels are round: avatars, presence dots, and channel marks use 50%. Servers are rounded squares: rail tiles at 12px, tightening to 10px on hover, with server badges at 30%.

Mascot avatars are the system's one playful form: a rounded square (28%), circle, hexagon, triangle, bean, or flower in a mascot color, with two white vertical pill eyes. The Huddle logo is a circle cut by vertical slots and renders in `currentColor`.

Motion is small and shared: 120ms for color and border changes, and `cubic-bezier(0.16, 1, 0.3, 1)` for rail pill height, tile radius, tooltip movement, and dialog entrances. A dialog and its backdrop fade in over 140ms, the dialog also from 98% scale, with no exit animation. Reduced motion removes transitions and that entrance.

## Components

### Buttons

Compact and plain, with fill as the only emphasis.

- **Shape:** Gently rounded (8px), 38px tall with 16px side padding, or 32px and 12px at small size. Access card buttons are full-width at 44px and 16px type.
- **Primary:** Indigo fill with white text, moving to Pressed Indigo on hover.
- **Secondary:** Secondary Fill with `text`, moving to its hover step.
- **Text:** No fill, underlined on hover.
- **Icon:** 32px square at 6px radius, `muted` turning `text` over the hover wash.
- **Focus / Disabled:** 2px Focus Ring at 2px offset; 50% opacity when disabled.

### Tags

- **Style:** 12px, weight 500, 1px by 6px padding, 4px radius, on Secondary Fill.
- **State:** The accent variant uses Mention Tint for mentions and the Admin role.

### Cards / Containers

- **Corner Style:** 8px, or 12px for dialogs.
- **Background:** Card Surface in the app, Raised Surface for dialogs, Access Card on the access field.
- **Shadow Strategy:** None in the app. See Elevation & Depth for dialogs and the access card.
- **Border:** 1px hairline in the app and on dialogs. The access card has none.
- **Internal Padding:** 16px in settings cards, 24px in dialogs, 32px on the access card (24px at 560px wide).

### Inputs / Fields

- **Style:** 40px tall, 8px radius, 12px side padding, with a `line-strong` border on the chat ground. Access card inputs drop the border for a filled `access-input` at 16px type.
- **Focus:** The Focus Ring plus an indigo border; access card inputs add a matching 1px ring.
- **Error / Disabled:** `danger` border, with a 1px danger ring on the access card when focused. Disabled inputs take `panel` and `muted`.

### Navigation

- **Rail:** Rounded-square tiles on the shell. The Home tile holds the Huddle logo, a separator follows it, then one tile of initials per room, then a Create room tile for admins and a Switch server tile when the app offers it. Hover and current fill indigo. The Home tile's red count is the total of unread direct messages. A room tile keeps the unread pill and adds the same red count for its unread mentions; its accessible name stays the room name and its description carries the numbers, such as `2 unread, 1 mention`. The rail scrolls with its scrollbar hidden, and one fixed tooltip follows the hovered or focused tile so scrolling never clips it.
- **Rail Pill:** A 4px marker in `strong` on the rail's left edge: 8px when unread, 20px on hover or focus, 32px when current.
- **Channel rows:** 32px rows of 15px type with an 18px hash icon, or a speaker for voice channels, in `muted`. Hover adds the wash and `text`; current takes `selected` and `strong`; unread takes `strong` and an 8px pill at the sidebar's edge. A channel with unread mentions adds a small red count at the row's end.
- **Direct message rows:** At Home, Members, and direct views the sidebar lists a "Direct Messages" section under Members, with a `New message` add button for everyone. Rows are 42px with a 24px avatar and the peer's name, and use the channel row states. The empty text is "No messages yet."
- **Section labels:** "Text Channels", "Voice Channels", and "Direct Messages" at 12px, with a small 24px add button.

## Do's and Don'ts

### Do:

- **Do** place every shell zone where a Discord user expects it: 72px rail, 248px sidebar, flexible chat, 240px member list, composer at the bottom, account panel floating across rail and sidebar.
- **Do** keep the Huddle name and logo on the rail and access screens.
- **Do** set messages flat and full width, with authors grouped and continuation lines flush.
- **Do** use the Rail Pill lengths exactly: 8px unread, 20px hover or focus, 32px current.
- **Do** read every color from the tokens in `src/chat.css` so both modes resolve, and define a new role in both.
- **Do** use Lucide line icons, mostly at 16 to 20px, in `muted`, moving to `text` or `strong` with state.
- **Do** build separation from hairlines and tonal steps before reaching for a shadow.
- **Do** draw the merged 32px window bar over every desktop screen, with traffic lights on macOS and drawn controls elsewhere.

### Don't:

- **Don't** use Discord's logo, illustrations, or proprietary font.
- **Don't** add controls for features that are not built, including voice calls, search, emoji, attachments, and presence.
- **Don't** show a presence dot anywhere but the account panel, or use it for anything but connection state.
- **Don't** put messages in bubbles or constrain the thread to a centered column. Home, setup, and settings may still use a readable column.
- **Don't** add breadcrumbs to the top bar.
- **Don't** uppercase labels in the shell.
- **Don't** add a resting shadow to the rail, sidebar, chat, member list, or account panel.
- **Don't** use a gradient outside the access field.
